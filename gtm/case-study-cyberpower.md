_Last updated: 2026-05-14_

## Draft A — Long Form (website / PDF)

**CyberPower: Full-Funnel Visibility Across $500K+ in Monthly Ad Spend**

---

**The Client**

CyberPower is one of the leading UPS and power protection brands on Amazon, competing across hundreds of SKUs in a category where buy box, pricing, and ad efficiency all move in lockstep. They run a full advertising mix — Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, and a programmatic DSP program — across both the US and Canadian marketplaces.

---

**The Challenge**

Managing $500K+ in monthly ad spend across four ad types and two marketplaces means a lot of data, a lot of reports, and a lot of manual work to stitch it into anything actionable. Before Calbridge, CyberPower's team was working out of native Amazon console reports — switching between Seller Central, Vendor Central, and the Advertising console to get anything close to a complete picture.

There was no single view of what their ad spend was actually producing across SP, SB, SD, and DSP together. And no way to see how advertising tied back to real margin.

---

**What We Built Together**

CyberPower connected all four ad programs to the Calbridge portal — SP-API (Seller Central and Vendor Central), Amazon Advertising API, and DSP. The full data pipeline went live in March 2026.

From day one, the team had:

- A unified dashboard showing SP, SB, SD, and DSP performance side-by-side — spend, sales, orders, ACoS, ROAS — refreshed every 6 hours
- US and Canadian marketplace data in isolated views with independent budget tracking
- Contribution margin analysis at the campaign level, factoring in COGS, FBA fees, and referral fees
- Automated insights flagging campaigns exceeding target ACoS, budget pacing issues, and scaling opportunities
- Vendor analytics: purchase orders, inventory forecasts across hundreds of ASINs, real-time sell-through

---

**The Results**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Monthly ad spend managed | $500K+ |
| Overall ACoS | ~12% |
| Overall ROAS | 8x+ |
| Ad types | SP, SB, SD, DSP |
| Marketplaces | US + Canada |

**By ad type:**

| Channel | ACoS | ROAS |
|---------|------|------|
| DSP | ~11% | ~9x |
| Sponsored Products | ~12% | ~8x |
| Sponsored Brands | ~14% | ~7x |
| Sponsored Display | ~11% | ~9x |

---

**What Changed**

Before Calbridge, getting a complete cross-channel view required pulling reports from three different Amazon interfaces and assembling them in a spreadsheet — every single week. Now it's one dashboard, updated automatically, with alerts when anything falls outside target ranges.

The team spends less time on reporting and more time on the decisions that actually move the needle.
