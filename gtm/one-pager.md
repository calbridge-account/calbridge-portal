## The Problem

Amazon agencies and brand teams are flying blind.

## What We Built

**Calbridge is a white-label Amazon analytics portal** built for agencies managing multiple brand accounts, and brands managing their own Amazon business.

It connects to Seller Central, Vendor Central, and Amazon Advertising to give you one place to see what's actually happening — and what to do about it.

---

## What It Does

**Unified Advertising Dashboard**
Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, and DSP — all in one view. No more switching between campaign manager tabs.

**Contribution Margin per ASIN**
The number that actually matters. Revenue minus COGS, minus FBA fees, minus referral fees, minus ad spend. You'll know immediately which products are making money and which ones are being subsidized by the ones that are.

**Automated Insights**
Flags campaigns running above target ACoS, ASINs losing money after fees, scaling opportunities, and anomalies — without you having to build the analysis every week.

**White-Label Client Portal**
Your clients log in and see your brand. Their data. Your platform. Built for agencies who want to look like they have proprietary infrastructure without building it.

**Vendor Central Support**
PO tracking, ship confirmations, net PPM, forecast compliance — alongside your advertising data. Most tools treat Vendor Central as an afterthought. We don't.
