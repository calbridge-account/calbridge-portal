### 💡 Positioning Insights for Outreach

**The Pain They Feel:**
1. Clients ask "where's my data?" every week → reporting is a time sink
2. Ad performance numbers don't show profitability (ROAS ≠ contribution margin)
3. Multiple brand clients = multiple dashboards = chaos
4. White-labeling a polished portal makes the agency look premium

**Calbridge Value Props by Persona:**

*For agency founders/owners:*
> "Turn your client reporting from a time drain into a premium deliverable. White-label Calbridge and give every client their own branded dashboard showing contribution margin, ad performance, and AI insights — without your team building it."

*For heads of account management:*
> "Stop exporting spreadsheets and building Looker Studio dashboards from scratch. Calbridge connects your Amazon data and serves it to clients in a clean portal under your agency's brand."

### 🔍 Contact Information Notes

- Most agencies don't publish direct email addresses on websites
- Best approach: LinkedIn DM to founder/CEO, or generic `hello@` / `info@` addresses
- Several founders have active personal LinkedIn profiles and are responsive to direct outreach
- Many have active YouTube channels / podcasts — referencing their content in outreach increases response rates

**Email pattern observed:** `firstname@agencyname.com` is common for boutique agencies

### ⚠️ Agencies to Avoid / Notes

- **Tinuiti** — too large ($1B+ revenue agency); Amazon is one of many divisions; unlikely to white-label
- **Pattern** — revenue-share model, not traditional agency; different motion
- **Thrasio** — acquirer, not agency
- **Publicis/WPP groups** — enterprise, not Calbridge ICP
- **Pure freelancers on Upwork/Fiverr** — below threshold for white-label portal ROI
- **eComEngine** — software tool, not agency
- **Pacvue** — technology platform, not agency (competitor-adjacent)

---

### 📌 Recommended Outreach Sequence

1. **LinkedIn connection + DM** to founder/CEO (reference their content or podcast)
2. **Email** `hello@` or `info@` with one-paragraph Calbridge pitch + Loom demo link
3. **Follow up** via LinkedIn after 5 days if no reply
4. **Twitter/X** if they're active there (several founders are)

**Subject line ideas:**
- "White-label client portal for [Agency Name]'s brand clients?"  
- "Your clients want contribution margin data — Calbridge delivers it under your brand"
- "Quick question about client reporting at [Agency Name]"
