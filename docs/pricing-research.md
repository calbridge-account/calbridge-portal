# CalBridge Pricing Research: Amazon Analytics & Profitability Dashboards
**Research Date:** March 24, 2026  
**Scope:** Competitor pricing, agency WTP signals, and recommended pricing for CalBridge

---

## Executive Summary

- **The market is bifurcated:** SMB tools (Sellerboard, Scale Insights) price at $19–$288/mo with transparent, self-serve pricing; enterprise/agency tools (Pacvue, Intentwise, Skai) are all custom-quote, starting around $500/mo and often $1,500–$5,000+/mo. CalBridge's agency positioning puts it squarely in the middle tier where prices range from $500–$2,000/mo per client portfolio.

- **% of ad spend is standard at the agency level.** Amazon PPC agencies typically charge clients 10–15% of ad spend for management, with some charging a flat retainer ($1,500–$3,000/mo) plus a smaller % (3–7%). SaaS tools like Teikametrics add 3% of ad spend over $10K/mo on top of their platform fee. Perpetua's Growth plan is base fee + % of ad spend.

- **Profitability analytics (CM1/CM2/CM3, TACoS) is heavily underserved.** Most tools focus on PPC automation; true contribution margin tracking with ad spend integration is rare. Sellerboard covers CM1/CM2 at a consumer price, but no multi-tenant agency portal exists at accessible price points. This is CalBridge's core differentiation.

- **Agencies pay $100–$500/mo per client account for analytics infrastructure** when using tools like Sellerboard multi-account access, and $500–$2,000+/mo for platforms like Intentwise or Pacvue at the agency tier. The gap between "good enough" ($79/mo Sellerboard) and "enterprise" ($2,000+/mo) is wide and underserved.

- **New-to-brand, revenue forecasting, and budget pacing are premium features** that drive upgrade decisions. Forums consistently show sellers willing to pay more for accurate forecasting and CM-level profitability that crosses ad spend with COGS and fees.

## Agency-Specific Tools & Pricing

### Tools Designed for Multi-Client Management

**Sellerboard (Agency Mode)**
- Sellerboard has an Agency section on their website and offers multi-account access
- Agencies can manage client accounts under a single login
- Pricing is per-client account (each client would need their own Sellerboard plan, $19–$79/mo)
- No white-label offering
- No consolidated multi-client dashboard; each account is viewed separately
- **Typical agency cost:** $50–$300/mo depending on number of clients and plans

**Intentwise (for Agencies)**
- Explicitly markets to agencies: "Manage clients and scale insights with unified data"
- Products include Ad Optimizer, Analytics Cloud, Product 360, AMC Explore, DSP
- Multi-client management built in
- Pricing: custom, requires demo/sales call
- Estimated $500–$2,000/mo per agency depending on number of managed accounts and modules
- No white-label

**Perpetua (Agency Partners)**
- Has formal agency partner program
- Growth plan ($695+% of ad spend) is the entry for agencies
- Premium plan for large agencies managing $500K+/mo in aggregate ad spend
- No publicly listed white-label option
- Customer success team included in Growth and Premium

**Pacvue (Agency Tier)**
- Has agency-specific sales process
- Avg contract ~$26K/yr for brands; agencies with multiple clients likely pay more
- Multi-client campaign management built in
- No public pricing; all negotiated

**Scale Insights (Agency Mode)**
- ASIN-based pricing allows agencies to scale without per-seat costs
- Aggregators and agencies explicitly called out as target market
- No multi-client dashboard — each account managed separately
- No white-label

**White-Label Options**
- No major tools in this space offer true white-label dashboards at accessible prices
- Some enterprise data platforms (like Intentwise Analytics Cloud) offer custom data integrations that could power white-label reporting via BI tools (Tableau, Looker)
- This is a significant gap — agencies building client-facing dashboards typically either:
  - Screenshot from their existing tools
  - Build custom reports in Google Sheets / Looker Studio
  - Use expensive BI platforms like Tableau ($70–$115/user/mo) on top of their data feeds

### Agency Fee Structures (How Agencies Charge Their Clients)

Amazon PPC agencies commonly use one or more of these models when billing their own clients:

| Model | Typical Rate | Best For |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| % of Ad Spend | 10–15% (range: 7–20%) | Mid-market to large advertisers |
| Flat Monthly Retainer | $1,000–$5,000/mo | Small-to-mid sellers, predictable budgets |
| Flat + % Hybrid | $500–$1,500/mo + 5–8% of ad spend | Most common for agencies managing $10K–$100K/mo in ad spend |
| Performance/Success Fee | 10–20% of incremental revenue | Less common; high-trust relationships |
| Hourly | $75–$200/hr | Ad hoc consultants, small accounts |

**Market standard for "% of ad spend" model:** 10–15% is the industry norm for full-service Amazon PPC agencies. Smaller agencies or newer clients often start at 12–15%. At higher volumes ($100K+/mo ad spend), rates typically compress to 7–10%.

**Minimum engagement fees:** Most agencies have minimum monthly fees of $1,000–$2,500 regardless of ad spend, to ensure the engagement is economically viable.

## Willingness to Pay Signals

### What Sellers Complain About (from forums, reviews, Reddit)

**Common pain points expressed in r/AmazonFBA, r/FulfillmentByAmazon, G2, Trustpilot:**

1. **"I can't tell if I'm actually profitable after ads"** — The most common pain point. Tools like Helium 10 and Sellerboard show gross margin, but TACoS (total advertising cost of sale, including organic) and true contribution margin after all variable costs (shipping, returns, referral fees, FBA fees) are rarely computed automatically.

2. **"My agency sends me reports I can't understand"** — Agency clients want clear, visual dashboards showing CM1/CM2 by ASIN, not just ROAS/ACoS in spreadsheets.

3. **"Perpetua restructured my campaigns and I lost 2 months of data"** — Common complaint. Users dislike black-box automation that disrupts existing campaign structures. Sellers want transparency.

4. **"Tools only care about ad spend, not profit"** — Multiple reviews note that the popular tools optimize toward ROAS, but don't consider the actual contribution margin of each product. Sellers with thin-margin products need CM-aware optimization.

5. **"I have 8 tools and none of them talk to each other"** — Fragmentation is a massive frustration. Data siloed between PPC tool, inventory tool, accounting tool, and Amazon reports.

6. **"The % of ad spend fee kills me as I scale"** — Teikametrics' 3% on ad spend is frequently cited as punishing growth. Sellers near the $10K threshold find the jump jarring.

7. **"Perpetua forces annual contracts and auto-renews"** — Cited in reviews as a con. Sellers want monthly optionality, especially early in adoption.

8. **"I pay $695/mo for Perpetua but still do reporting in spreadsheets"** — Reporting/dashboard quality at the mid-market tier ($500–$800/mo) leaves much to be desired.

### Features Sellers Say They'd Pay More For

1. **Accurate TACoS tracking** (organic + paid, blended) — "If it could show me true TACoS by ASIN, I'd pay double" (from r/AmazonFBA thread)
2. **Revenue forecasting by ASIN** — Inventory/cash flow planning is a major pain
3. **Budget pacing with alerts** — "I need to know when I'm 70% through budget with 40% of the month left"
4. **Contribution margin by product** — CM1/CM2/CM3 is gold standard. Very few tools do this properly.
5. **New-to-brand (NTB) tracking** — Premium metric for brand-building vs. repeat buyers
6. **Multi-account agency view** — "I manage 12 brands and need one dashboard to show them all"
7. **Client-facing reporting** — Agencies specifically want branded, clean dashboards they can share with clients without sharing tool access

### What's Considered "Too Expensive" vs "Worth It"

| Price Point | Sentiment |
|------------|-----------|
| $19–$39/mo | Almost universally "worth it" for basic profit tracking |
| $79–$99/mo | Considered reasonable for established sellers doing $200K+/yr GMV |

**Common reasons for cancellation:**
- Not using enough features to justify the price
- Found a cheaper tool that covers the core use case
- Switched to in-house management or an agency
- "It didn't help my bottom line" — hard to attribute ROI
- Forced annual contracts with limited flexibility

- **Pain:** Client reporting is manual; no consolidated view across clients; can't demonstrate value clearly
- **CalBridge opportunity:** Strongest here. Agencies would pay $500–$1,500/mo for a multi-client portal with CM1/CM2/CM3, TACoS, and client-facing dashboards. The white-label positioning unlocks further premium pricing.

## % of Ad Spend Model Analysis

### Is Anyone Pricing on % of Ad Spend?

Yes — several tools use it:

| Tool | % of Ad Spend Component |
|------|------------------------|
| Teikametrics | 3% on ad spend over $10K/mo (on top of $99–$999/mo flat) |
| Perpetua Growth | % of ad spend above base $695/mo (exact % not publicly disclosed; estimated 2–4%) |
| Scale Insights (1% Plan) | 1% of total ad spend for unlimited ASINs (custom plan) |
| Pacvue | Estimated 0–3% of ad spend (custom negotiated) |
| Amazon PPC agencies | 7–15% of ad spend (for human management services) |

### Market Standard for Amazon PPC Agency Fees (% of Ad Spend)

- **Standard range:** 10–15% of monthly managed ad spend
- **Floor:** ~7% for very large accounts ($500K+/mo ad spend)
- **Ceiling:** ~20% for small accounts or specialized agencies
- **Industry norm for SaaS tools:** 1–3% of managed ad spend (if using % model)
- **Hybrid (most common):** Flat retainer ($500–$1,500/mo) + 3–7% of ad spend

**CalBridge Assessment:** A % of ad spend model could work but creates revenue unpredictability for the customer and makes CAC calculation harder. A flat tiered model (by number of client accounts or revenue range) is more predictable and preferred at the agency-software level.

## Recommended Pricing for CalBridge

### Positioning Summary

CalBridge is a **multi-tenant client portal for Amazon agencies** that connects Advertising (SP/SB/SD/DSP) and SP-API, computes CM1/CM2/CM3, TACoS, revenue forecasting, budget pacing, and NTB metrics.

It sits **above** Sellerboard (too simple, no multi-tenancy) and **below** Pacvue/Intentwise (too expensive, too enterprise). It targets the **agency sweet spot** where no current tool delivers the full suite cleanly.

### Recommended Pricing Tiers

#### Tier 1: Agency Starter — **$499/mo** (or $399/mo billed annually)
- Up to **5 managed client accounts**
- CM1/CM2 calculation per client
- TACoS dashboard (organic + paid blended)
- Budget pacing alerts
- Basic revenue trend view
- Standard Amazon SP-API + Advertising API connections
- 1 user (agency owner/operator)
- No white-label

**Rationale:** Anchors below Perpetua's Essentials ($695/mo). Agencies managing 5 brands at $200–$500K/yr each are typically billing $3,000–$15,000/mo and can easily justify $499/mo.

#### Tier 2: Agency Pro — **$999/mo** (or $799/mo billed annually)
- Up to **15 managed client accounts**
- Full CM1/CM2/CM3 with COGS management
- TACoS + true contribution margin by ASIN
- Revenue forecasting (30/60/90 day)
- New-to-brand (NTB) tracking
- Budget pacing with custom thresholds and alerts
- DSP integration
- **Client-facing portal** (agency-branded; each client sees only their data)
- Up to 5 user seats
- Email/chat support

**Rationale:** This is the value tier. Agencies billing $5K–$30K/mo to clients would pay $999/mo without blinking. Intentwise's agency tier likely runs $1,500+/mo without the consolidated reporting. This is CalBridge's "hero" plan.

### Alternative: Per-Brand Pricing (Consider for Early Growth)

Instead of tiered agency plans, a per-managed-brand model could work:

- **$149/mo per active client account** (includes CM1/CM2, TACoS, pacing, forecasting)
- **Volume discounts:** 5+ clients → $119/mo, 10+ → $99/mo, 20+ → $79/mo
- **Agency overhead fee:** $99/mo flat (for multi-client dashboard access)

**Pro:** Scales naturally with agency growth. Easier to sell ("you only pay for what you manage").  
**Con:** Revenue is unpredictable; agencies may have seasonal fluctuations in client count.

---

### Pricing Model Recommendation

**Recommended approach:** Hybrid tiered flat model (Tier 1–3 above) with annual discount (20%). 

- Avoid % of ad spend — adds complexity and unpredictability
- Consider a **14-day free trial** with full Tier 1 access to reduce friction
- Lock in early agencies with a **Founder Pricing** discount (e.g., 40% off for life for first 50 agency customers)
- Sell to agencies primarily through **partner/referral channels** and LinkedIn — avoid consumer SaaS channels
