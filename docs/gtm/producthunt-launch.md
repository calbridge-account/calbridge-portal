## Long Maker Comment (600-800 words)

Hey Product Hunt 👋

I'm Abe, the founder of Calbridge — and I want to be upfront about why I built this: I needed it.

I manage around $500,000 a month in Amazon advertising across brands including CyberPower and Acer. For years, I paid for the tools everyone pays for — Perpetua, Teikametrics, Pacvue. And for years, I had the same experience: beautiful dashboards, detailed recommendations, and then me, manually going into Seller Central to actually make the changes.

That's not automation. That's a very expensive to-do list.

So I started building Calbridge about two years ago, initially for myself. No VC pitch. No "we're disrupting adtech" deck. Just: I have a problem, I'll build a solution.

**Here's what Calbridge actually does:**

**AI Bid Optimization That Writes Back to Amazon**
This is the core thing. Most tools tell you what to change. Calbridge changes it. The AI analyzes your campaign performance across all ad types (Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, DSP) and pushes bid adjustments directly to Amazon via the API. You set the guardrails. It does the work.

**Contribution Margin Tracking (CM1 / CM2 / CM3)**
ACoS is a useful metric. It's also incomplete. CM1 covers ad costs, CM2 adds COGS, CM3 adds overhead. Seeing those three numbers per campaign completely changes how you prioritize spend. I built it in because I needed it. It's there.

**Campaign Creation Wizard**
You can now build and launch new Amazon campaigns directly from Calbridge — pushed to Amazon, no Seller Central required. Select your ad type, set your targeting and bids, go live. For anyone managing multiple brands, the context-switching alone is worth it.

**Report Builder with PDF Export**
Custom performance reports, exportable as white-label PDFs. If you report to clients, stakeholders, or just want something cleaner than a Seller Central screenshot, this is the feature you've been waiting for.

**White-Label Agency Portal**
Agencies can run all their client accounts under their own branding. Multi-brand switcher built in. Your logo, your colors, your clients — one place.

**Pricing (and why I made it month-to-month):**
- Free — get in, explore, connect your account
- Starter — $99/mo
- Growth — $249/mo
- Pro — $499/mo
- Agency — $549/mo + $299/brand

No annual contracts. No cancellation fees. No collections calls (yes, that's a real thing a competitor has done). If Calbridge stops working for you, you leave. It should be that simple.

**Who this is for:**
- Amazon sellers managing $50k+/mo in ad spend who want real automation, not alerts
- Agencies running multiple client accounts who need white-label reporting and a clean multi-client interface
- Operators who are sick of paying for dashboards and doing the work themselves anyway

I'm here all day to answer questions. Ask me anything — about the product, the tech, how the AI model works, why Amazon ad tools tend to be bad, whatever. Happy to go deep.

And if you're currently mid-contract with one of the incumbents and want to start a free account to see how it compares — calbridge.ai is open right now.

Thanks for the support. This one's been a long time coming.
