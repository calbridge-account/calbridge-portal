## What You'll See

The dashboard is built around a **contribution margin waterfall** — CM1, CM2, and CM3. If those terms are new to you, here's what they mean in plain English:

- **CM1** — Revenue minus the cost of goods and Amazon fees. This is your gross profit before you touch advertising.
- **CM2** — CM1 minus your ad spend. This is what you actually made after paying for ads. It's the number that tells you if your campaigns are generating real profit or just revenue.
- **CM3** — CM2 minus any additional overhead (storage, returns, chargebacks). Your true bottom line at the product level.

Most Amazon reporting stops at ROAS or ACOS and calls it a day. Those metrics matter, but they don't tell you whether you made money. The CM waterfall does.

**You'll also see:**
- TACOS (Total Advertising Cost of Sale — ad spend as a % of total revenue, not just ad-attributed revenue)
- ROAS and ACOS by campaign
- Revenue forecasting against your monthly targets
- Budget pacing — so you know before the 25th if you're on track or drifting
- New-to-brand metrics — how much of your growth is coming from new customers vs. repeat
