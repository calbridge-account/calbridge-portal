## What's Inside

**Connected Data Sources**
- Amazon Advertising API (Sponsored Products, Brands, Display)
- Selling Partner API (SP-API) for revenue, returns, inventory, and fees
- Amazon DSP for programmatic visibility
- OAuth connection flow — clients self-connect in under 5 minutes

**Contribution Margin Waterfall (CM1 / CM2 / CM3)**
- CM1: Revenue minus COGS and Amazon fees
- CM2: CM1 minus ad spend — the real profitability signal
- CM3: CM2 minus overhead — true bottom-line by product or account
- Replaces the spreadsheet your team builds manually every month

**Performance Metrics**
- TACOS, ACOS, ROAS — across all campaign types
- New-to-brand (NTB) metrics — growth quality, not just growth
- Budget pacing — know mid-month if a client is on track or burning fast

**Forecasting & Planning**
- Revenue forecasting against monthly targets
- Ad spend pacing by account and portfolio
- Variance alerts before they become client calls

**Client Portal**
- White-label login for clients (white-label on Pro/Scale plans)
- Configurable views — show clients what's relevant, keep internal data internal
- Reduces back-and-forth reporting requests
